## Summary

Sander Hoogendoorn shares a practical, no-hype view of AI at iBOOD: how it’s reshaping product architecture, engineering, and team culture. He covers real risks like lock-in and fragile dependencies, and explains why autonomous microteams, experimentation, and adaptability replace traditional processes in the AI era.
